## Context

My capstone project at Kaplan Business School, for a case-study client in the energy sector. The client had run AI pilots, but they kept stalling before they reached production.

## The problem

Four barriers held the pilots back, and each one made the others harder to clear:

- Legacy integration
- Security approval
- Regulatory evidence
- Unmeasured benefits
